About the mission

The covers the far north of Luzon — the Cagayan Valley, Isabela, Nueva Vizcaya, the Cordillera highlands and out to the Batanes islands. It is rice country and river country, hemmed in by mountains on both sides, and Tuguegarao City itself has a reputation for being one of the hottest places in the Philippines.

Missionaries there speak along with the local languages — Ilocano and Ibanag are widely spoken in the valley. Every six weeks, at what is called a transfer, missionaries may be moved to a new area and assigned a new companion. When that happens, this page changes.
