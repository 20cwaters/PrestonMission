The short version

He loves hearing from people

How to write him

Email — the easy way

Missionaries get one preparation day a week, and that is when they read and answer email. Write any day you like; he will see it on his next P-day.

Email him

Letters and packages

Physical mail goes to the mission office, and they pass it along at transfers or zone conferences, so give it a few weeks. Packages to the Philippines can be slow and are sometimes taxed — small, flat, and cheap travels best.

A few tips. Keep it upbeat and newsy — he only gets a few hours to read and reply. Tell him what is happening at home, ask about the people he is teaching, and send pictures. Do not be discouraged if the reply is short; some weeks the internet café closes early.
