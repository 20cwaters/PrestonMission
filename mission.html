About the mission

The covers the far northeast of Luzon: all of Cagayan, most of Isabela, Kalinga up in the Cordillera mountains, and the Batanes and Babuyan islands out to sea, almost all the way to Taiwan. It was created in July 2024, so it is one of the newest missions in the Philippines.

It is rice country and river country. The Cagayan River, the longest in the Philippines, runs up the middle of the valley between two mountain ranges, and Tuguegarao City itself has a reputation for being one of the hottest places in the country.

Missionaries there speak along with the local languages. Ilocano and Ibanag are widely spoken in the valley.

How a mission works

What is the MTC?

The Missionary Training Center. Missionaries learning a new language usually spend about six weeks at the one in Provo, Utah, then continue at a training center in their assigned country — for Preston, that is the one in Manila — before heading out to their actual mission.

What is a transfer?

Roughly every six weeks the mission president reassigns missionaries. Preston may stay put or be moved to a new town, and he may get a new companion either way. Transfer news usually shows up in the weekly email, and the Where He Is page gets updated when it does.

What is a companion?

Missionaries are always paired up. Companions live, study, and teach together for the length of a transfer or longer. You will see a lot of names come and go over two years.

What is a P-day?

Preparation day — one day a week for laundry, groceries, sightseeing, and writing home. That is when the weekly email arrives, and when he is allowed to call or video chat with his family.
